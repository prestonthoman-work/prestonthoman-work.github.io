Orbit Fling Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the Orbit Fling iPhone game (“Orbit Fling”, “we”) handles. In short: your game stays on your phone, we don’t run accounts or collect analytics, and the only outside service is Google AdMob, which shows the ads.

What stays on your phone

Orbit Fling saves your game in a file on your device: the courier name and badge you choose, your route progress, stars and best times, tips and cosmetics, game statistics (such as launches and deliveries), and your settings. This file is never sent to us. There are no accounts or logins, and we don’t run any servers.

Like other app data, the save may be included in your iPhone’s own backups (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. We don’t collect analytics, crash reports, contacts, location or any personal information, and we don’t sell or share data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Orbit Fling is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: an ad between some routes, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for bonus tips. To show and measure these ads, Google’s software in the app may collect information such as your device’s IP address, device and app information, an approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

No tracking prompt. Orbit Fling never asks permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites, so Google can’t use your device’s advertising identifier.

EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland. If you’re in one of these regions, the game asks for your consent before ads are shown, using Google’s consent message. If you choose “Do not consent”, you still play exactly the same game; Google shows limited ads that don’t use your personal data for personalisation. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

Children

Orbit Fling is made for a general audience and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

To erase everything the game has saved, use Settings → Erase all progress, or delete the app. For information Google holds about ads, use Google’s tools linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
